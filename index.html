We started Bad Cop because every rack looked the same and so did every fit. Nobody asked us to be loud. We just got tired of being quiet.

Every piece is made in small runs, cut oversized, and finished with a little red where you least expect it. Wear it to class, to the gig, to the 2 a.m. maggi run.

The zipper is the whole idea. Pull it down and see who you are when nobody's watching.

Why Bad Cop?

Most clothes are made to disappear. We make pieces you can actually point to, in numbers small enough that not everyone gets one.

Numbered, not mass-made

Every piece runs in an edition of 50 to 100. Once it sells out, it is gone for good.

Heavy fabric, proper cut

14oz denim and 240gsm cotton, cut to sit the way they are meant to, not the way the trend says.

Loud where it counts

Red stitching, red prints, and a zip that says you were never going to blend in.

Don't ask twice

Pieces are numbered and released in small batches. Follow along on Instagram for the next one, or message us on WhatsApp with questions.
